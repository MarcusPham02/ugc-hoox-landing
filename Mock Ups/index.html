Frequently Asked Questions

What types of social media platforms does UGC Hoox support?

UGC Hoox supports all major social media platforms including Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, and Twitter. Our AI is trained to analyze content optimized for each platform's unique characteristics and best practices.

How accurate is the AI analysis?

Our AI has been trained on thousands of high-performing UGC posts and provides insights with over 90% accuracy. We continuously improve our algorithms based on the latest content trends and user feedback.

Can I use UGC Hoox for commercial purposes?

Absolutely! Both Free and Premium tiers can be used for commercial content creation. Many businesses, influencers, and marketing agencies use UGC Hoox to optimize their content strategy and improve engagement.

What's the difference between Free and Premium?

Premium offers significantly more value: 15 daily analyses (vs 5), support for multiple social media accounts (vs 1), advanced hook analysis, personalized improvement suggestions, priority customer support, and access to a comprehensive content analytics dashboard.

How quickly do I get analysis results?

Analysis is instant! Results appear within 2-3 seconds of submission. Our AI processes your content in real-time, providing immediate feedback so you can iterate and improve your content on the spot.
