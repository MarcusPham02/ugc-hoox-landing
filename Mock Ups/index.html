Frequently Asked Questions

What UGC Hoox does — and what’s coming next

What does UGC Hoox actually do?

You tell it the brand you want to work with and a bit about yourself, and it writes a tailored outreach pitch — a hook, why you’re a fit, and a clear ask — ready to send from your own email or DMs.

Do I have to find the brands myself?

For now, yes — you bring the brand you already want, and UGC Hoox writes the pitch. Brand discovery (suggesting who to reach out to) is on the roadmap, not in the first release.

Will the pitches actually sound like me?

That’s the point. You set your niche, tone, and best results once, and every pitch is written in your voice — not a generic template a brand has seen a hundred times. You can edit and regenerate before you send.

What about hook scoring and the content calendar?

They’re coming. We’re launching with the pitch writer first — the hardest part of getting paid — then adding AI hook scoring and a batch-content calendar so you can plan and deliver the work you land.

How much will it cost, and how do I get in?

Pricing isn’t locked in yet. Join the waitlist below for early access — you’ll be first to try it and help shape what launches.
